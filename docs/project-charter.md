# CampusConnect Project Charter

## Purpose
The scenario describes placement management through spreadsheets, email attachments and WhatsApp follow-ups. CampusConnect provides a web-based prototype to centralise applications, placement status, employer feedback and reporting.

## Initial scope
Student opportunity discovery/application; employer opportunity/application workflow; placement tracking; employer feedback; coordinator reporting.

## Stakeholders
Students, employers, placement coordinators, academic supervisors and administrators.

## Methodology
Scrum is used because the broad problem is understood while workflows and priorities may evolve after early screens and stakeholder feedback.

## Out of scope
Live institutional identity, real student records, production Microsoft 365 tenant access and legally binding institutional records.
