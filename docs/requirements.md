# Requirements

## Users
Students; employers; placement coordinators; academic supervisors; administrators.

## Core workflows
1. Student profile/CV
2. Browse opportunities
3. Submit and track applications
4. Employer opportunity management
5. Employer application review
6. Placement creation and lifecycle tracking
7. Supervisor assignment and progress review
8. Employer feedback
9. Notifications
10. Reports
11. Notices
12. Audit/governance
13. Administrative reference data and role management

All prototype records are synthetic. The product must not be presented as a live institutional system without institutional authorisation and technical integration.
