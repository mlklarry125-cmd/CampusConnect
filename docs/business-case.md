# Business Case

The project package uses planning assumptions for the fictional GBC scenario. They must be validated against real institutional time logs, volumes, rates, implementation quotations and adoption evidence before financial commitment.

Headline planning assumptions supplied for the package:
- Year 0 investment: BWP 361,950
- Annual benefit at full adoption: BWP 315,060
- Staff time affected: approximately 2,500 hours/year
- Three-year NPV at 12%: BWP 44,084
- Payback: 2.3 years
- Low-adoption scenario at 60% benefits: NPV BWP -187,274

Adoption is a central sensitivity in the planning model.
